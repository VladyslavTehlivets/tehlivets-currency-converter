# Upload refinement · Revision 6.1

The approved Revision 6 designs stay in place. This refinement designs their file controls.
The five existing upload fields use soft nested bezels, product colors, and a designed chooser.
The component includes selection, drag feedback, preview, removal, validation, progress, interruption, and completion states.
The design system shows the shared component for each product.
FamilyAssistant accepts JPEG, PNG, WebP, and HEIC images up to 10 MB.
Teplo accepts the existing audio and video types, with a 200 MB single-upload limit.
The local prototype demonstrates progress. Production must use actual transfer progress and existing server validation.
No font changes are included.
